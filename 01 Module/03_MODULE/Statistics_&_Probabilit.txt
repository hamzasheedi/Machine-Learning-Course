
Statistics & Probability for AI/ML
Module Overview
Statistics and Probability are the core reasoning tools of Artificial Intelligence. While programming tells a computer what to do, and mathematics tells it how to compute, statistics and probability teach machines how to make decisions under uncertainty.
In real-world AI:
Data is incomplete and noisy
Patterns are probabilistic, not guaranteed
Predictions must include confidence
Decisions are based on likelihood, not certainty
This module builds the statistical thinking required to design, evaluate, and trust AI systems.

Why Statistics & Probability Are Essential for AI/ML
ML models are trained on samples, not entire populations
Predictions are probabilities, not facts
Model evaluation is statistical testing
Bayesian models rely entirely on probability
AI systems must handle uncertainty and risk
Without statistics, models cannot generalize.
Without probability, models cannot reason.

Learning Philosophy
This module follows an intuition → visualization → application approach:
Understand what the concept means
Visualize how it behaves
Apply it inside ML workflows
